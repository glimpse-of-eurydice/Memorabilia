# Primitive philosophy

> **Status:** conceptual orientation, not an implementation contract
> **Researcher-only:** this document must never enter a subject workspace.

## Why primitives at all?

Memorabilia asks how differently ordered encounters can become constitutive of
an artificial agent system. Its primitives are deliberately small external
dynamics. They do not encode personality, value, identity, agency, or a theory
of the whole mind. Those are possible patterns to investigate, not admissible
starting answers.

> A good primitive is not one that explains everything.
> A good primitive is one that leaves something genuinely capable of emerging.

The three families describe a minimal temporal grammar:

```text
something participates
        ↓
participating things can become related
        ↓
some consequences remain available, weaken, or return
        ↺
```

## Selection / Activation

Selection asks what from prior organization participates in the present
encounter. Without selection, every retained trace is equally implicated in
every moment and developmental history has little local structure.

Selection is not transformer attention and does not claim access to hidden
model cognition. It is an observable boundary in the external scaffold. Its
formal implementation may involve an agent proposal, a deterministic runtime
rule, or both; choosing among these is a Layer A research-design decision.

## Association

Association asks what becomes related—or more strongly related—when structures
participate together. It makes repeated conjunction capable of modifying later
organization instead of leaving only a sequence of isolated records.

Association does not certify semantic truth, and the runtime should not invent
the meaning of a relation merely because two items co-occur. Semantic labels
and mechanical strengthening must remain distinguishable in provenance.

## Persistence / Decay / Reactivation

Persistence asks which consequences of an encounter remain available across
developmental steps. Decay gives non-use a consequence; reactivation lets a
partial cue return weakened organization to functional availability.

This family is not a mandate to permanently delete old records. A trace can
remain in provenance while ceasing to dominate ordinary retrieval. The
distinction between retained record and current functional availability is
central to the project.

## What the project does and does not claim

The primitives are inspired by questions from engram research and dynamic
systems approaches to development. They are not asserted to be biological
primitives, discovered mechanisms inside the base model, or a sufficient
account of cognition.

Thought Space is an externalized observational surface. A bubble is not an
engram, an edge is not a synapse, and a graph is not the agent's mind. The
object under study is the base model plus its external, instrumentable
scaffold—and whether the scaffold's accumulated history changes later use.

The primitive set earns continued use through reproducible functional
contribution and experimental identifiability, not through intuitive or
neuroscientific resemblance alone.
